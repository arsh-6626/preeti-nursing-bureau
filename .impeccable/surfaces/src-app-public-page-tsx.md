# Surface brief: public website (redesign 2026-10-12)

Scope: every public route (home, services, service detail, about, contact, legal, request wizard, request status) plus the public header, footer and mobile action bar. Visitor mode: Persuade (the request wizard is Operate inside it). The admin is out of scope and keeps its current design.

Audience: worried families in Delhi NCR, often on phones, often older. Action: start a care request or call / WhatsApp. Proof on hand: none beyond product facts (two shift lengths, six NCR cities, manual payments, human matching). Constraint: no invented claims, people, figures or reviews; stock photos only if clearly generic (none used in this pass).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A funded medtech brand for home care: the confidence and product polish of Hims / Function Health / Forward, built around a real 3D object (the bureau's care carton) and working controls. Refuses the white-card template, the icon-tile row and every invented trust signal.

OWN-WORLD: Ink (#061a1d) stages for hero, shifts and closing; cool paper (#f4f7f5) for reading sections; deep teal brand (#0b6b61); signal mint (#7cf2d2) only on ink as the single accent. Geist display set very large with tight tracking; Geist Mono for small data labels (shift lengths, references, step numbers). 28px panels, pill buttons and chips, 14px inputs. Soft mint glows on ink, no other gradients.

STORY: Visitors understand in one viewport that this is home care arranged by people, in 12 or 24 hour shifts, in Delhi NCR; they can explore services and the two shifts directly, see exactly how the request moves, and start a request or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed ink stage under a floating pill nav. Left: headline in white at display scale, one sentence, mint pill "Find a Caregiver" plus Call/WhatsApp. Right: the 3D carton floating on a mint glow with capsules. On phones the carton sits between headline and text.

FORM: User-pinned canon at funded-startup craft (owner rejected two concept rolls and asked to "look like a real startup", then asked for the 3D carton hero); seed key ff76ec3d (canon). Signature interactions: pointer-following 3D carton; service explorer tabs; 12 h / 24 h segmented switch; scroll-lit process steps.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
