# Surface brief: public website

Scope: public marketing pages (home, services, about, contact, legal) and the multi-step care request. Visitor mode: Persuade (request flow is Operate inside it). Admin dashboard is a separate Operate surface that shares tokens only.

Audience: worried family members in Delhi NCR, often on a phone, often older. Action: start a care request or call / WhatsApp. Proof on hand: none (no testimonials, credentials, counts). Constraint: only 12-hour and 24-hour shifts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed with more care than the category: a calm, trust-first home-care site whose real action (start a request) is working in the first viewport. It refuses the stock-nurse photo hero, the icon-tile card row, and every invented trust number.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, cool-neutral section fields, navy headings and ink, muted teal for every primary action and selection, subtle green reserved for confirmed or positive state. One sans family (Figtree) with tabular numerals for references, dates and rupees. Hairline borders, 12px panels, 10px controls, soft tinted shadow only on the request panel.

STORY: The visitor understands what the bureau arranges, that a person will call before anything is confirmed, and that only 12 or 24 hour arrangements exist. They start a request or call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: navy two-line headline, one sentence, call and WhatsApp actions. Right (below on mobile): a raised request panel with service select and the two shift faces side by side, continuing into the full form.

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by the owner over the roll; seed key 5c4336cc.
